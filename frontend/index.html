⚠️ Responsible use

This system is a demonstration of facial identification technology and should not be used for surveillance, law enforcement, access control, or high-impact decisions without appropriate safeguards. It is trained on a small, self-supplied image set and cannot identify every Indian cricketer — only the people represented in its training data.

Limits & safeguards

        
    
          
  • Recognises only people present in the training dataset — everyone else is Unknown.
    • 
          
  • Refuses to answer below the confidence threshold, and when the top two classes are too close.
    • 
          
  • No demographic, emotion, age or gender inference. No identity search across photos.
    • 
          
  • Metrics come from a small self-supplied dataset; augmentation can leak between splits, so reported accuracy is optimistic.
    • 
          
  • Performance collapses with low light, extreme angles, occlusion (helmet, cap, mask), motion blur or heavy compression.
    • 
          
  • Not for surveillance, law enforcement, access control, hiring or any high-impact decision.
    • 
          
  • Face images are sensitive personal data: follow your local law (e.g. India's DPDP Act) and delete data/uploads when you are done.
